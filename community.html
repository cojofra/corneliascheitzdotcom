Community

No one builds a career alone.

Many of the opportunities, lessons, and turning points in my own journey have been made possible through communities, mentors, colleagues, and people willing to share their experiences and perspectives.

That is why I believe strongly in creating spaces where people can connect, learn from one another, and support future generations. Whether through alumni engagement, mentorship, storytelling, or advocacy for women in technology, I see community as an essential part of leadership.
