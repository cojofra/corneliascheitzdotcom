Talks & Events

Conversations about leadership, technology,
and the human side of change.

Drawing on experience across science, technology, and leadership, I speak on topics that sit at the intersection of organizational transformation, systems thinking, innovation, and the human dimensions of change.

Over the years, I have contributed to conferences, universities, leadership forums, and industry events, bringing perspectives shaped by both research and executive practice. My approach emphasizes thoughtful dialogue and practical insight, helping audiences make sense of complexity and navigate change with greater clarity and confidence.
