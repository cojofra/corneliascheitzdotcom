#PassTheMic

Everyone has a story worth sharing.

Throughout my career, I have been fortunate to learn from remarkable people. Some became mentors, some colleagues, and some simply crossed my path at the right moment.

Behind every title are moments of doubt, unexpected opportunities, difficult decisions, and lessons learned along the way. By creating space for these conversations, I hope to amplify diverse voices, celebrate different career paths, and encourage others to embrace their own journeys with curiosity and confidence.

This series is not about success stories — it's about people, growth, and the experiences that shape us.
